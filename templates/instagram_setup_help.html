Instagram Setup Help

📱 Step 1: Convert to Business Account

Your Instagram account must be a Business account to use our bot features.

  1. Open Instagram app
  2. Go to Settings → Account
  3. Tap "Switch to Business Account"
  4. Follow the setup process

🔗 Step 2: Connect to Facebook Page

Your Instagram Business account must be connected to a Facebook Page.

  1. Go to Settings → Account → Linked Accounts
  2. Tap "Facebook"
  3. Create a Facebook Page or connect existing one
  4. Complete the connection

✅ Step 3: Connect to Chata

Once your account is set up correctly:

  1. Go to your Chata dashboard
  2. Click "Connect New Account"
  3. Log into your Facebook account
  4. Authorize Chata to access your Instagram

❓ Troubleshooting

Error: "No Instagram Business account found"

Make sure your Instagram account is:

  • Set to Business type (not Personal or Creator)
  • Connected to a Facebook Page
  • Linked to the Facebook account you're logging in with

Error: "Access denied"

This usually means:

  • Your Instagram account isn't a Business account
  • Your Instagram isn't connected to a Facebook Page
  • You're logging into the wrong Facebook account
