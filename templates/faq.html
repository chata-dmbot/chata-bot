Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Chata and how it works

Do I need technical skills or coding to use Chata?

Not at all! Chata is designed to be super simple. Just connect your Instagram account, define your AI model’s personality with a few prompts, set your preferences, and you're ready to go. No coding or technical knowledge required - if you can use Instagram, you can use Chata.

What makes Chata different from other auto-reply tools?

Traditional auto-reply tools rely on rigid, scripted flows. Chata uses a personality-driven AI model that learns your voice and responds naturally to real messages—no flow building. You can fine-tune tone and boundaries, and test it with 100 free replies before you commit.

Can I control how the AI speaks?

Yes. You define your AI model’s personality, tone, and boundaries using simple prompts and examples—no coding required. You can adjust it anytime and your changes show up immediately in new conversations.

Will the AI sound like a chatbot?

No. Chata generates replies dynamically from your personality settings and real-world examples, so conversations feel like a natural human chat, not a rigid scripted flow.

How does Chata work?

Chata connects to your Instagram account through Meta's official API. When someone sends you a DM, our personality-driven AI model analyzes the message and responds using your personalized settings. Think of it as having a smart assistant who knows exactly how you talk and can handle conversations just like you would.

Is it safe to connect my Instagram account?

Absolutely! We use Instagram's official API and Meta's secure authentication system. We never store your password and only access the permissions you explicitly grant. Your account security is our top priority - we take this very seriously.

Can I use Chata for multiple Instagram accounts?

Yes! Our Pro and Creator plans support multiple Instagram accounts. You can set up different personalities and settings for each account, making it perfect for managing multiple brands or personal accounts. Each account gets its own unique voice.

How much does it cost?

We offer three simple pricing tiers: Starter ($29/month), Creator ($49/month), and Pro ($99/month). All plans include a 7-day free trial, and you can cancel anytime. Contact us for full details on our pricing plans.

Can I try Chata before paying?

Yes! We offer a free demo that you can try right now. Just click the "Try the Demo" button to see how Chata works with sample conversations. We also offer a 7-day free trial on all paid plans, so you can test it with your real account.

What kind of support do you offer?

We provide email support for all users. Contact us if you need help connecting an account, configuring the assistant, or managing your plan.

Is my data private and secure?

We protect data in transit with TLS/SSL, store passwords as secure one-way hashes, and restrict application and administrative access. We use service providers only where needed to operate Chata, as explained in our Privacy Policy. You can request deletion of your data at any time.
